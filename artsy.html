Artsy Escape 🎨

Color, creativity, and inspiration. These destinations are havens for art lovers, dreamers, and anyone seeking a fresh perspective.

Paris

The city of light and art. Paris dazzles with its galleries, street art, and bohemian neighborhoods.

Best time: April–June, September–November

Vienna

Elegant palaces, classical music, and vibrant street art. Vienna is a city where tradition and creativity meet.

Best time: April–May, September–October

Oaxaca

Colorful markets, folk art, and a creative culinary scene. Oaxaca is a feast for the senses and the soul.

Best time: October–March

Florence

Renaissance masterpieces, artisan workshops, and golden sunsets. Florence is a living gallery for art and culture lovers.

Best time: May–September

Lisbon

Colorful tiles, creative neighborhoods, and Atlantic views. Lisbon’s energy sparks inspiration at every turn.

Best time: March–May, September–October
